menu_book Synopsis

High above the mist-choked valley of Oakhaven stands the Whispering Spire, a monolithic structure carved from fallen celestial obsidian. Scholar-apprentice Mira has spent her quiet youth deciphering the muffled murmurs echoing from its foundational vaults. When a crimson eclipse tears open the celestial veil, the dormant gargoyles awaken with prophecies of ancient kings. Bound by a forbidden contract of blood-ash, Mira must ascend thirty-three spiraling floors before the solstice bell tolls, or watch her homeland collapse into perpetual twilight.
